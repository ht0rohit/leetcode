# Insights Log

Durable observations and recognition cues, accumulated over time as you solve
new problems and revisit old ones. Not a rehash of solutions -- the point is
the cue that tells you "this is that kind of problem."

Format per entry:

```markdown
## <pattern or topic>

- **Recognition cue**: what in the problem statement/constraints signals this
- **Common pitfall**: what trips people up here
- **Related problems**: links
```
